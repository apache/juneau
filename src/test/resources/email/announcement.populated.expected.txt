The Apache Juneau team is pleased to announce the release of Apache Juneau 9.2.1.

This patch release fixes a regression in the REST client and refreshes dependencies.

- Fixed a NullPointerException in RestClient
- Upgraded Jetty to 12.0.x

Known issues:
- The OpenAPI 3.1 parser does not yet support webhooks.

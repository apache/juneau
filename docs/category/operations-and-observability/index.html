Operations and Observability

Observability, metrics and tracing bridges, execution statistics, and logging and management surfaces.

📄️ 3.3.44. Observability — Micrometer + OpenTelemetry

Observability is opt-in and disabled by default. A fresh RestContext built with no MetricsRecorder or TracerHook bean registered never records a metric sample, never opens a span, and never adds a per-request cost — even when io.micrometeropentelemetry-api are sitting on the classpath. Opt in by registering a @Bean MetricsRecorder and / or @Bean TracerHook on the resource. Same non-negotiable contract as Jakarta Bean Validation.
